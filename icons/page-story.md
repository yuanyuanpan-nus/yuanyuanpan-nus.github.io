# Academic Page Story of Yuanyuan Pan

## About Me

![Portrait](avatar.jpg)

Welcome to my Home Page! I am a Ph.D. candidate in Finance at the National University of Singapore (NUS) Business School. 

My research interests are Household Finance, Investments, and Behavioral Finance.

I am supervised by Prof. [Allaudeen Hameed](https://discovery.nus.edu.sg/220-allaudeen-hameed) and Prof. [Karsten Müller](https://www.karstenmueller.com/). I was a Visiting Ph.D. Student at the University of Southern California in 2025 supervised by Prof. [David Hirshleifer](https://sites.uci.edu/dhirshle/). 

I hold an M.S. in Statistics from NUS and a B.S. in Mathematics from Soochow University.

**I am on the 2026-2027 job market.**
